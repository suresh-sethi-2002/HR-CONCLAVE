About HR Conclave

Where industry and academia meet.

GIET’s HR Conclave brings HR professionals, industry representatives and academic leaders together to discuss how work is changing and what that means for the next generation of talent.

The forum connects emerging HR practices and talent management with workforce development. Discussions and professional networking give educators and employers a place to share what they’re learning.

The 2026 theme, program and event details are provisional.
